Frequently Asked Questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name at the top, then Subscriptions, then Forged, then Cancel Subscription. You can also tap "Manage Subscription" inside Forged's Settings to go there directly. Your access continues until the end of your current billing period.

How accurate is the AI food scan?

Forged's AI scan is one of the most accurate on the App Store, especially when you add a short text description of what you're eating (like "egg cheese bean burrito"). The description tells the AI what's hidden inside wraps, mixed dishes, and homemade meals. For packaged foods, use the barcode or label scanner — those pull verified nutrition facts directly.

My calorie targets changed — why?

If you've been logging your weight regularly, Forged automatically reviews your weight trend and adjusts your daily calorie target based on whether you're hitting your goal. This is intentional — static targets don't work as your body changes. If you'd rather set your own numbers and have them stay locked, turn on Custom Macros in the Adjust screen and Forged will respect them.

Is my health data private?

Yes. Your data is stored securely and is never sold or used for advertising. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from Settings → Delete Account. Full details in our Privacy Policy.

How do I get a refund?

Subscription refunds are handled by Apple, not Forged. Open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Forged, then "Report a Problem" — or go to reportaproblem.apple.com. Apple decides refund eligibility per their policies.
